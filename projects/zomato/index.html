About Zomato and the Dataset

The exploration begins with Zomato, a household name in India and beyond. Zomato isn’t just a food delivery app; it’s a portal that lets users delve into the heart of Indian dining culture. With its vast repository of restaurant information, user reviews, and menu details, Zomato is the gateway to discovering the perfect dining spot.

The creation of this Zomato dataset aimed to address the scarcity of comprehensive, up-to-date datasets covering all Indian cities. This dataset provides the latest information up to March 2022, making it a definitive resource for food enthusiasts, researchers, or anyone curious about India’s culinary diversity. Explore these datasets to uncover hidden gems and trends within the Indian food and entertainment industry. Bon appétit!

Zomato Bangalore Restaurants 2022

This tabular dataset consists of all restaurants in Bangalore. Bangalore is one of the metro countries of India and is also one of the favorite spots for foodies with a variety of cuisines and street food available. The city of students, working professionals, and youth filling up every space, Bangalore is the location to be a foodie, and Zomato taking the highest orders in the country also comes from Bangalore.This data has ratings and review details, the cuisines available, the approximate price for two persons, etc.

  • The data was scraped from Zomato Bangalore.
  • Around 10k rows of data was fetched.
  • Around 20 different available features for the dataset.
